# Supplementary Material for the paper "Kinship networks, kinlessness and supportive friendship at different ages. Evidence from Italy in 1998 and 2016"

This repository contains the Supplementary Material associated with the article “Kinship networks, kinlessness and supportive friendship at different ages. Evidence from Italy in 1998 and 2016”.

It includes seven Excel workbooks, corresponding to Figures 1–7 and the related tables, with results organized by year (1998 and 2016), sex (males, females, and both sexes combined), age group, and kinship category, where applicable.

The files report the estimates presented in the Supplementary Material together with their 84% confidence intervals. This repository is intended to support the transparency, reproducibility, and verification of the reported results.
